Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use NTChat? You can view public pages without an account. Registration is required to join rooms, post messages, react to content, send private messages, and use private pages.
Can I create my own room? Yes. Authenticated users can create rooms, assign a category, add tags, and manage their own room content.
Can rooms require an invite? Yes. Room owners and staff can make a room invite-only so users need an invitation before they can join.
How do private messages work? You can search for another profile and start a direct conversation that only the two participants can open.
How do notifications work? Notifications are created when room activity affects members, such as new messages, room updates, or room invitations.
Can staff manage the platform outside Django admin? Yes. Staff members have access to a dedicated staff panel with moderation controls, room access settings, profile management, and broadcast notification tools.
